Hello! My name is John Miller. I am a NSF Graduate Research Fellow and a first-year Biomathematics PhD student at Florida State University. My research explores tumor development and the emergence of metastatic conditions using ecological and game-theoretic models.

I graduated _summa cum laude_ from North Carolina State University in May 2026 with B.S. degrees in Applied Mathematics and Biological Sciences. While there, I was a [Goodnight Scholar](https://goodnight.ncsu.edu).
